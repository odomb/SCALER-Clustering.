# SCALER-Clustering.
"Clustering learners by job profiles and CTC using Manual Flagging and Unsupervised ML (K-Means &amp; Hierarchical) to identify top-tier industry talent."

This project performs profiling and clustering of Scaler learners based on
**company, job position, CTC, and experience** to identify:

- High-earning professionals & top performers
- Salary benchmarking across companies & job roles
- Experience-based pay growth patterns
- Role-wise earning potential for career guidance

Both **Manual Clustering** and **Unsupervised Clustering**
(K-Means & Hierarchical) approaches are implemented.

---

##  Business Problem

Scaler wants to:

1. Identify companies & roles where learners achieve **higher salary outcomes**
2. Benchmark compensation across:
   - Company
   - Job Position
   - Experience Levels
3. Detect high-potential candidate clusters for:
   - Career mentoring
   - Placement targeting
   - Salary negotiation guidance

This analysis supports **data-driven career recommendations**.
